Lock-out / Tag-out instructions, by NFC

The shutdown steps, in the language you read.

    How it works

    Tap, read, act. Approve once, update anytime.

    Two sides of one tag: what the technician does at the machine, and what the machine owner does before the tag goes live.

    At the machine.

    The tag is placed where the work starts.

    Tap the tag. Read the procedure. Work safely.

    Each machine has its own NFC tag. A technician taps the tag with a phone and immediately receives the correct, machine-specific Lock-out/Tag-out instructions.

    1. Tag on the machine

      The NFC tag links to the correct procedure.

    2. Tap your phone

      The correct instructions open immediately.

    3. Read your language

      The approved procedure appears in the selected language.
