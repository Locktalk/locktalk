How LockTalk works

Four steps: a tag on the machine, a tap with your phone, the procedure in your language, and live help when something is unclear.

One tag per machine

The tag never changes

When a procedure changes, the owner updates it online. The tag stays on the machine and the next tap shows the new version. Nothing is reprinted.

Approved first

Reviewed before it goes live

AI drafts the translations. The machine owner approves every language. Technicians only ever see approved text.

Live help

Ask on the spot

A supervisor and a technician can talk in their own languages, with the original wording visible next to the translation.

Lock-out / Tag-out instructions, by NFC

The shutdown steps, in the language you read.

LockTalk puts machine-specific Lock-out/Tag-out instructions on an NFC tag fixed to the machine. A technician taps the mobile device on the tag and reads the approved procedure in their own language.

    How it works

    Tap, read, act. Approve once, update anytime.

    Two sides of one tag: what the technician does at the machine, and what the machine owner does before the tag goes live.

    At the machine.

    The tag is placed where the work starts.

    Tap the tag. Read the procedure. Work safely.

    Each machine has its own NFC tag. A technician taps the tag with a mobile device and immediately receives the correct, machine-specific Lock-out/Tag-out instructions.

    1. Tag on the machine

      The NFC tag links to the correct procedure.

    2. Tap your device

      The correct instructions open immediately.

    3. Read your language

      The approved procedure appears in the selected language.

    Behind the tag.

    See how one machine-specific procedure is translated, reviewed and approved before technicians can access it.

    Only owner-approved translations are published. Draft translations are never shown to technicians at the machine.

    What it does

    One machine procedure, available in multiple languages.

    LockTalk connects every machine to its own digital lock-out/tag-out procedure. Technicians can read approved instructions and ask questions in their preferred language.

    Everything needed at the machine.

    The procedure stays connected to the machine while the content, languages and approved versions can be managed online.

    MACHINE-SPECIFIC

    The correct procedure

    Every NFC tag opens the lock-out/tag-out procedure for that specific machine. Technicians do not need to search through folders or select a machine manually.

    APPROVED TRANSLATION

    Instructions in your language

    AI prepares the translations, but the machine owner reviews and approves every language before it is published.

    TWO-WAY COMMUNICATION

    Ask when something is unclear

    Technicians and supervisors can communicate in their own languages while the original message remains visible.

    ALWAYS CURRENT

    Update without replacing the tag

    When a procedure changes, the owner updates it online. The NFC tag remains on the machine and the next tap opens the latest approved version.

    Who it is for

    Built for manufacturing teams that do not all speak the same language.

    LockTalk supports the people who create, manage and follow lock-out/tag-out procedures, from safety leads and production managers to international technicians working at the machine.

    Different roles, one clear procedure.

    Each role uses LockTalk differently, but everyone works from the same approved, machine-specific procedure.

    01 Creates and approves

    Safety and maintenance leads

    Create the original machine procedure, review translated versions and decide which languages can be published.

    • Manage one procedure per machine
    • Review translated safety instructions
    • Approve updates before publication
    Result: control over every published procedure
    02 Manages the workplace

    Plant and production managers

    Help internal teams, contractors and external technicians access consistent instructions at the correct machine.

    • Support multilingual teams
    • Reduce searching for documents
    • Keep current procedures accessible
    Result: consistent information across the factory floor
    03 Uses the procedure

    International technicians

    Tap the machine’s NFC tag and read the approved shutdown procedure in a familiar language before starting work.

    • Open the correct machine procedure
    • Read approved steps in a preferred language
    • Ask a supervisor when something is unclear
    Result: clearer instructions at the point of work
